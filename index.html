The Making

Five fittings.
One garment.

Nothing here is rushed. From first measure to final press, a single garment passes through five stages of hand work.

01

Consultation

We talk cloth, occasion, and silhouette before a tape ever comes out.

02

Measure

Thirty-two points taken by hand, recorded to the eighth of an inch.

03

Cut

A paper pattern is drafted and chalked directly onto your chosen cloth.

04

Baste

A loosely hand-sewn skeleton is fitted to your body and adjusted live.

05

Finish

Final stitching, pressing, and a last fitting before the garment is yours.
